How it works

From blank page to a finished song in three moves.

  1. 1

    Start with a spark

    Pick a style and Creer writes drums, bass, chords, texture and a melody for a whole section. Or choose one sound and begin from nothing.

  2. 2

    Shape it your way

    Draw notes in the piano roll, drop in loops, play it live on your keyboard or a MIDI controller, then balance everything in the mixer.

  3. 3

    Share the result

    Export a WAV file to send to anyone, or save the project and pick it up later. Everything stays on your device.

Spark an idea

Never stare at an empty timeline again.

Press Idea and choose a mood — lo-fi, house, ambient, cinematic, pop or jazz. Creer writes a whole section in your key and tempo, adds it to matching tracks and names the section. Don't like it? Undo is one step.

  • Six styles that write in your song's key
  • Lands on matching tracks, ready to edit
  • Start playing immediately, or place it at the playhead

Play it, don't just program it

A keyboard that's always within reach.

Type on your computer keys, click the on-screen piano or plug in a MIDI keyboard. Arm a track, hit record and your performance lands in the arrangement. Fifty-three synthesized sounds, each with its own voice controls.

  • Musical typing and Web MIDI input with velocity and sustain
  • Octave, attack, tone and dynamics per track
  • Every sound auditions before you commit to it

Mix with intent

Levels, space and polish — all in the same window.

A full mixer with faders, pan and reverb and delay sends, plus an insert chain on every track and the master: filter, drive, EQ, chorus, phaser, tremolo, auto-pan, delay, reverb, compressor and bitcrusher.

  • Per-track and master insert effects
  • Swing from straight time to full triplet feel
  • Exports match what you hear, effects and all

Built for flow

Everything is one keystroke away.

Press Ctrl K (or ⌘ K) and search any action, sound or setting. Add a bass, jump to bar 33, set the key to D minor or type a tempo like 124. Right-click clips for the usual edits, and press F to give the editor the whole screen.

Everywhere you are

Powerful where you want it, simple where you don't.

Creer Mobile

Open it on a phone and it switches to a touch-first studio with a multi-touch piano, chord strip, drum pads and finger-sized editing.

Private by design

Projects autosave to your own device. There are no accounts, no servers storing your songs and nothing to upload.

One-file portable

Download Creer.html — the entire studio in a single file you can double-click, keep on a USB stick and run offline.

Loop library

Beats, basslines, chords, melodies and textures that drop onto the right track and transpose to your key automatically.

Real piano roll

Draw, drag, resize, quantize and nudge notes. Box-select, zoom up to 800% and hear every pitch as you move it.

Open source

Creer is released under the Unlicense — public domain. Read it, fork it, bend it into your own instrument.
